## Video prompt

```text

Explain the purpose of sending alerts from an IoT application and compare the

API credentials, exact endpoint formats, costs, privacy guarantees, or
delivery guarantees. Mention safe handling of secrets where relevant without
repeating any actual credentials. End with a decision guide for selecting a
channel and a short setup checklist.

provider or API details that may have changed.
```
